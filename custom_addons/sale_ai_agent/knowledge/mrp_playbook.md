# Manufacturing & Costing Playbook

## 1. Comprehensive Work Center Hourly Cost Formula
$$\text{Cost/Hour} = \text{Labor} + \text{Power} + \text{Depreciation} + \text{Facilities Allocation} + \text{Maintenance}$$

Where:
- **Labor**: $\text{Workers Count} \times \text{Hourly Wage}$
- **Power**: $\text{Machine kW} \times \text{Electricity Tariff (\$/kWh)}$
- **Depreciation**: $\frac{\text{Asset Value}}{\text{Estimated Operational Hours}}$
- **Facilities Allocation**: $\frac{\text{Monthly Rent \& Utilities}}{\text{Productive Operating Hours}}$

## 2. Multi-Level BOM Cost Rollup
$$\text{Unit Cost} = \sum (\text{Component Qty} \times \text{Standard Cost}) + \sum (\text{Operation Hours} \times \text{Work Center Hourly Rate})$$

## 3. Margin Target Pricing
$$\text{Suggested Sale Price} = \frac{\text{Total Unit Cost}}{1 - \text{Target Margin \%}}$$
